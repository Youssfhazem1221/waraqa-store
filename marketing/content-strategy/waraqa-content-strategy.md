## Content pillars

### 1. Paper Science for Artists (searchable, product-led)
Educates on the exact spec (gsm, acid-free) that differentiates Waraqa from marketplace generic sketchbooks — directly extends the "what to check before you buy" section already written for the buyer's-guide page.
**Spokes:** "What does GSM mean for sketchbooks?" · "Acid-free paper: does it actually matter?" · "Why markers bleed through cheap sketchbooks (and how to avoid it)."

### 2. Buying & Comparison Guides (searchable, high commercial intent)
The hub here is the two pages already drafted in `marketing/competitors/waraqa-competitor-analysis.md` ("Waraqa vs. Marketplace Sketchbooks" and "Best Sketchbooks in Egypt"). This pillar's job is to build the spokes around it.
**Spokes:** "Sketchbook sizes explained: A5 vs A4 vs square" · "Best sketchbook for beginners in Egypt" · "Spiral vs. hardcover: which lasts longer for travel sketching?"

### 3. Egyptian Sketching Life (shareable, audience-led)
Builds brand affinity and shareability rather than chasing search volume directly — the Godin/Handley-style pillar. Also the most likely to earn organic social shares given no social presence currently exists in the codebase.
**Spokes:** "Where to sketch outdoors in Cairo: 7 spots artists love" · "Egyptian sketch artists worth following" · gift-guide content around creative/journaling occasions.

### 4. Behind the Paper (trust-building, E-E-A-T)
Fills a real gap: the codebase currently has **no testimonials, founder bio, production photos, or founding story** beyond generic copy. This pillar doubles as content *and* the missing trust assets the SEO audit flags.
**Spokes:** "How Waraqa sketchbooks are made in Cairo" · "Why we chose kraft covers over glossy binders" · a founder/maker story piece.
