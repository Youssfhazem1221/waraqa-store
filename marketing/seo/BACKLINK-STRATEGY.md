# Waraqa — Backlink Strategy

**Written:** 2026-10-05 · **Domain:** waraqa.art (live since 2026-10-03)

No backlink data source is configured for this site, so there is no baseline
count here. The first step below creates one. Treat every target in this file
as a lead to check, not a guaranteed link.

## The principle

For a new domain, ten links from real Egyptian art, design and lifestyle sites
are worth more than a thousand from anywhere else. Google judges a store like
this on whether people *in its market* point to it. That rules out bought links,
link packages, private blog networks, mass link swaps and comment spam: a new
domain caught doing any of these can be held back for months, and recovery costs
far more than the links were worth.

What Waraqa has that others will want to link to:

1. **The paper guide** (`/paper-guide`, EN + AR) — the only plain-language gsm
   guide aimed at Egyptian buyers. This is the page to point every outreach at.
2. **Being made in Cairo** — local-brand roundups and gift guides need exactly
   this.
3. **Physical product** — a sketchbook in an artist's hands is an easy yes for
   collaborations.

## Step 0 — Baseline (this week, 30 minutes)

- [ ] Verify **Google Search Console** for the *domain* property `waraqa.art`
      (DNS TXT record; Cloudflare adds it in one click). Submit
      `https://waraqa.art/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Verify **Bing Webmaster Tools** (it can import the property from Search
      Console). Bing also feeds ChatGPT search and Copilot.
- [ ] Note the referring-domain count from both. That is the number to grow.

## Step 1 — Foundations (week 1–2)

These are low-authority but tell Google the brand is real, and they are the
links every legitimate business has:

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** as a service-area business (Cairo, Giza,
      delivery Egypt-wide), website = `https://waraqa.art/ar` for Arabic searchers.
- [ ] **Bing Places** (imports from Business Profile).
- [ ] Website link in the **Instagram**, **Facebook** and **TikTok** bios. These
      are nofollow, but they bind the social profiles to the site (the
      Organization schema already lists Instagram in `sameAs`).
- [ ] Egyptian business directories that list real shops — check each is still
      live and indexed before spending time on it.

## Step 2 — Community (month 1–2)

The highest-value links, because they come from the exact audience.

- [ ] **Urban Sketchers Cairo / Cairo Sketchers** (~14k members per the market
      deep-dive). Offer sketchbooks as prizes for a sketch crawl, or a
      discount code for members. Ask that the event post or page link to
      `/paper-guide` or the shop.
- [ ] **Art and design faculties.** Helwan University's Faculty of Fine Arts
      (Zamalek), the Faculties of Applied Arts, Alexandria's Fine Arts faculty,
      and design departments at private universities. A student discount
      offered through a student club or society usually earns a link on its
      page or a pinned post. Time it for September–October and February intake.
- [ ] **Art teachers and studios** that publish supply lists. Ask to be the
      listed "where to buy" for the sketchbook line.

## Step 3 — Creators (ongoing, 1–2 a month)

- [ ] Send sketchbooks to Egyptian illustrators and sketchers with portfolio
      sites, Behance or Linktree pages (the deep-dive lists candidates). Ask for
      a credit link in the Behance project or a mention with a link, never for
      a paid "dofollow" link.
- [ ] An **artist edition** or co-designed cover gives both sides a reason to
      link: a page on waraqa.art about the artist, and the artist's own
      announcement linking back.
- [ ] Offer guest posts in the other direction: invite an artist to write for
      the Waraqa blog. They will link to their own feature.

## Step 4 — Press and roundups (pitch 4–6 weeks ahead)

- [ ] **Egyptian lifestyle and culture media** that run "local brands" and gift
      features (for example CairoScene, Scoop Empire, Egyptian Streets, Cairo
      360). Pitch angle: *Cairo-made sketchbooks priced for students, with a
      free bilingual paper guide.* Attach product photos and the founder story.
- [ ] **Gift guides**: back-to-school (August), Ramadan, and end-of-year. Editors
      plan weeks in advance.
- [ ] **Expert-quote platforms** (Qwoted, Featured and similar): answer
      journalist questions on drawing, creativity and art supplies.

## Step 5 — Stockists and events

- [ ] **Stockists**: bookshops, concept stores and art cafés that carry the
      sketchbooks usually list brands on a "brands we carry" page.
- [ ] **Markets and festivals**: craft bazaars, design weeks and comics
      festivals publish exhibitor lists that link to each vendor's site. Use the
      `waraqa.art` URL on every application form.

## Step 6 — Turn mentions into links (monthly, 15 minutes)

- [ ] Search for "Waraqa sketchbook", "ورقة سكتش بوك" and the Instagram handle.
      Where someone mentioned the brand without linking, thank them and ask for
      the link.

## Where links should point

| Page | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `/paper-guide` | Educators, communities, press: the most link-worthy page |
| `/blog/what-to-draw-in-cairo` | Cairo culture and lifestyle sites |
| `/shop` | Stockists, gift guides, directories |
| `/about` | Press features and founder stories |
| `/ar/...` | Any Arabic-language site: link the Arabic URL |

## What to measure

- **Referring domains** in Search Console ▸ Links and Bing ▸ Backlinks, monthly.
  Count sites, not links.
- **Non-brand impressions** in Search Console for "sketchbook", "سكتش بوك" and
  "دفتر رسم" (the audit's leading indicator).
- A link that brings no visits and comes from a site unrelated to art, design,
  Egypt or lifestyle is not worth chasing.
