0What this changes from prior work

Three findings here update the earlier competitor, pricing, and SEO audits already in marketing/.

1. Waraqa is priced below the market's own validated sweet spot (240–340 EGP) — only the A4 Mixed Media (250 EGP) reaches it.

2. Bozoor (بذور), not Jumia, is the real direct competitor — an indie D2C brand at 125–350 EGP with chronic stockouts on its core SKUs (a validated unmet-demand signal).

3. Waraqa's flat 50 EGP shipping charge is likely below real courier cost (85–150 EGP per the report) — see §11 for what this does to unit economics.

2Growth & demand signals

  • Egypt e-commerce GMV: USD 9.91B (2025) → USD 11.49B (2026E), 10.0–11.89% CAGR through 2031.
  • Mobile = 72.5–75% of transactions. Social commerce ≈ 40% of online retail — not a minor channel.
  • Search split: دفتر رسم 72%, "sketchbook" 23%, "art journal" 5% nationwide — but near parity between Arabic and English in affluent Cairo/Alex zones, raising the stakes on the bilingual-crawlability gap already flagged in the SEO audit.
  • Bimodal seasonality: Peak 1 Sept–Oct (back-to-school), Peak 2 May–June (graduation portfolios). Today is Aug 20 — Peak 1 is 2–6 weeks out.
  • Diwan Bookstore is actively expanding stationery/journal shelf space into 6th of October City and El Gouna — premium paper goods have room in physical retail too, not just online.

4Target customer & buying journey

Age 18–32, Greater Cairo (48% of national e-commerce) + Alexandria (14%), B/A/upper-middle income. Discovery via Reels/TikTok → evaluate on GSM, texture, lay-flat binding → buy via DM/social commerce or COD checkout.

Recurring pain points

  1. Media bleeding & cockling — sub-140gsm paper marketed as "mixed media." Waraqa's floor is 150gsm on every SKU — already clear of this failure line.
  2. Fragile binding — perfect-bound spines crack; spiral notebooks deform in bags.
  3. Import inflation — Canson/Fabriano/Moleskine priced out of reach after EGP devaluation.

7Sourcing & currency risk

  • Hybrid model: import fine-art paper (140–300gsm, acid-free/cotton-blend from Europe/East Asia) + domestic converting/binding.
  • Domestic mills (Rakta, Misr Edfu, Qena) only produce low-gsm newsprint/carton — fine-art stock must be imported.
  • Converter MOQ: 500kg–1 metric ton per grade — directly explains why small-batch costs more per unit than the report's at-scale benchmark.
  • Real lay-flat needs Smyth-sewn hardcover or Wire-O double-spiral — not standard perfect binding.
  • 65–75% of COGS is FX-exposed. A 10% EGP depreciation → ~6.5–7.5% COGS increase.

Report benchmark (at-scale, 280 EGP retail): COGS ≈ 90–110 (35–40% of retail) → 60–65% gross margin. This is unlikely to apply directly to Waraqa's small batch — see §11 for an adjusted scenario.

Recommendations

  1. Get real COGS from your supplier — everything above is a structured guess until then.
  2. Confirm the actual courier rate paid vs. the 50 EGP charged.
  3. Don't run paid ads against single-item cheap SKUs (especially the Mini) until a bundle exists.
  4. Hold off on marketplace listings until margin is confirmed.
  5. Build a small FX buffer into the next production run's pricing.

12Sales strategy

Zero-cost first (start now)

  • Reactivate Instagram — 3,000 dormant followers, zero CAC to re-engage.
  • Launch TikTok — no presence today, best-validated format for this audience.
  • Join and genuinely participate in Students of Fine & Applied Arts (~95k) and Cairo Sketchers (~14k). Skip building a new Facebook Page.

Low-cost, inventory-aware seeding (next 2–3 weeks)

  • Seed 6–10 units (not the report's 50 — that's over half of current stock) to students/creators, drawn from abundant SKUs (Mini, Kraft), not the scarce A5-150/A4-320.

Hold until real numbers exist

  • Paid Meta/TikTok acquisition — likely loss-making on single-item orders today (§11).
  • Marketplace listings — referral fees eat an unconfirmed margin.

Pricing sequencing

  1. Don't reprice current live stock — trust cost outweighs the margin gain.
  2. Price the next production run inside 240–340 EGP, especially 250–320gsm SKUs.
  3. Test one new SKU at the 400–650 EGP specialized-watercolor tier — a test, not a lineup replacement.

14Bottom line

Three structural tailwinds converge right now:

  1. Import cost dislocation pushed Canson/Moleskine to 800–2,400+ EGP — Waraqa's 150–320gsm range sits inside the gap they vacated.
  2. Bozoor's stockouts are a validated demand signal, not a hypothesis — the closest competitor can't supply its own core SKUs.
  3. Waraqa is underpriced relative to its own specs — a low-risk opportunity that needs no new product, only correct pricing on the next batch.

Resolve these two internal unknowns before spending on paid acquisition:

  • Real COGS per SKU
  • Actual courier cost vs. the 50 EGP charged

Two things being split, not one

Equity (ownership) — permanent, doesn't shrink because someone's away. Active operator compensation — variable, paid only to whoever is actively working that period. Conflating the two is the most common way this kind of arrangement goes wrong; keeping them separate is what lets Abdelfattah earn something real without being paid the same as the two of you doing the daily work.
